Common questions.

Is the bid tool actually free?

Yes. The web demo at /tool.html is free — no signup, no credit card. We’ll save the anonymized bid (no photo, no email) so our calibration gets better. Paid plans unlock saved bid history, shared price books, and the SMS quote bot.

How accurate is it?

We target ±10% on a clear whole-pile photo. The bid always comes back as a range plus a recommended quote — never a single number — so you can adjust for stuff the photo can’t see (the back of the garage, the basement stairs). Calibration improves every week as more operators submit bids.

Does it handle weird loads — mattresses, fridges, paint?

That’s the whole point. The vision model is trained to flag the items that wreck a bid when you miss them: mattresses, freon appliances, electronics, tires, paint/hazmat. You can also check those off manually before submitting.

What if my ZIP isn’t covered?

We have curated dump-fee data for the top 50 US metros. Outside those, we use the national average (~$25/yd³). Paid plans let you override with your actual local transfer station rate.

Will this replace my FSM (Jobber, Housecall)?

No. Tonnage is the pricing brain that sits in front of your existing tools. Bid with Tonnage, then send the job to Jobber for scheduling and invoicing. (We’ll have a Jobber integration in Q3 2026.)
